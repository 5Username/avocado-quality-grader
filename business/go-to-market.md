# Go-to-Market & Money Plan

## Phase 1 – Launch (Week 1-2)
- Deploy this GitHub repo to GitHub Pages or Netlify (free)
- Add real M-Pesa payment (Daraja API + callback)
- Post daily on X targeting:
  - #AvocadoKenya
  - #KenyaFarmers
  - Cooperatives in Murang'a, Kiambu, Meru, Trans Nzoia
- Offer free first analysis to 50 farmers for testimonials

## Phase 2 – Scale (Month 1)
- WhatsApp Business API version (highest conversion in Kenya)
- Bulk pricing for cooperatives: KES 40/photo or monthly subscription
- Partner with 2-3 exporters for white-label grading

## Phase 3 – Revenue Targets
| Month | Photos/day | Revenue |
|-------|------------|---------|
| 1     | 20         | KES 30k |
| 2     | 50         | KES 75k |

## Tools Integration
- **Grok AI**: Advanced photo analysis when simple JS is not enough
- **GitHub**: Source of truth + free hosting
- **Gmail**: Payment receipts + weekly quality reports to farmers
- **X**: Primary customer acquisition channel
